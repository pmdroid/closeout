# Landing

Mode: persuade.

Audience: someone deciding whether an agent's change can be accepted, and someone building the orchestrator that checks that decision.

Action: open the specification, or adopt the runner.

Proof: the draft in spec/0.1.md, the four schemas, and an example beforePR board using this repository's own requirement ids.

Constraints: sentence case headings, no eyebrow, no card grid, no gradient text, body contrast at least 4.5:1, self-hosted fonts. No invented customers, benchmarks, or host name.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first screen is a departure board of one acceptance decision. It refuses the cream-paper homepage with a serif headline and a row of cards.

OWN-WORLD: Matte black flap faces, paint-white letters, steel rules, amber on a held row, dim red on a blocked row. Condensed gothic for flap letters, a grotesque for reading, a mono only inside code. The cell is the only container.

STORY: The visitor sees a gate blocked because the review has no evidence, believes the policy file is the requirement, and opens the specification or the runner.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The board fills the width. Navigation sits on the steel frame. The heading is the destination line. Four requirement rows follow, then the word blocked, then two actions on the lower rail.

FORM: Seed 3c37fcf2. A continuity script was the sixth grounded candidate. The split-flap concourse won audience identification and product clarity, and that world is the build. Signature motion is one staggered flap settle on load.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
